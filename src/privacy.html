Privacy policy

Draft, to be confirmed by Becca (the items marked "to be confirmed" need her answers)

This explains what The Sweetest Bakery ("we", "us") does with your personal information, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We keep it to the minimum we need to bake and deliver your order.

Who is responsible

The Sweetest Bakery, run by Becca Smith. Contact us on WhatsApp: . Becca acts as the information officer.

What we collect, and why

  • When you send an order (Cart or Custom Order): your name, phone number, what you ordered, the date you need it, collection or delivery, and your notes (for example allergies). We use this to prepare your order and to contact you about it. We save a copy online so Becca can find it, and we also put the same details in the WhatsApp message you choose to send.
  • If you attach an inspiration photo to a custom order: the picture (made smaller on your phone first). It is stored privately and only Becca can open it. It is deleted when the order is deleted. Please do not send photos of people, especially children.
  • If you leave a review: your name, optional stars and your words. Nothing is shown on the website until Becca approves it, and your phone number is never part of a review.
  • If you create an account: your email address, a username, an optional small profile picture and a password (we never see or store your password in readable form; our sign-in provider keeps it protected). Your past orders then show under My account. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address.
  • On your own phone or computer only: your cart, and, if you leave "Remember my details on this device" ticked, your name, phone, collection / delivery choice and allergies so you do not retype them. These stay in your browser; they are not sent to us until you send an order. Use "Forget my details" to remove them.

We do not take card details on this website. We do not sell your information and we do not send marketing messages unless you clearly ask us to.

Where it is kept, and who helps us

  • Supabase hosts our online database and sign-in. Data region: to be confirmed by Becca (shown in the Supabase project settings).
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries the order message you send; its own privacy policy applies to that message.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts for the site, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads. Google also handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google".

How long we keep it

Orders are kept for the bakery's business records. Your account stays until you delete it. When you delete your account, your login and profile are removed and the name, phone number and notes are taken off your past orders (the order items and total stay in our books without your name).

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. You can edit your profile and delete your account yourself under My account, or message us on WhatsApp. If you are unhappy, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

Children

Accounts are for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to order for you.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page. Last updated: November 2026.
